Adaptation for graphicsdemoskeleton (2026): shared HLSL, native DirectX 11,
DirectX 12 and Vulkan hosts, embedded shaders, explicit resource bindings and
synchronization, wind-driven clouds, a free-fly camera with world-space cloud
and fog intersections, and 120 FPS pacing. The original GLSL and its full
credits are in Original/.
